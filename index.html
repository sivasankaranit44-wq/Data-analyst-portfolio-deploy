Projects

Online store sales pipeline

I took nine raw files from a Brazilian online store, about 1.5 million rows in total, cleaned them step by step in SQL Server, and built a three-page Power BI dashboard on top.

  • Sales jumped about 7 times on Black Friday 2017, then settled back the next month.
  • Health and beauty is the top earning category, ahead of watches and gifts.
  • Orders arrive well before the promised date: about 9 days in São Paulo, about 28 in the slowest state.

Tools: SQL Server, Python, Power BI

Bank transactions pipeline

I took eight raw files from a real Czech bank, about 1.1 million rows in total, cleaned them step by step in SQL Server, checked the numbers at every step, and built a Power BI dashboard on top. Dates stored as text became real dates and Czech labels became English, with no rows or money lost along the way.

  • About three quarters of all the money moved was cash deposits and cash withdrawals, far ahead of transfers.
  • 76 of the 682 loans, about 11 percent, are bad: 31 finished without being fully paid and 45 are running in debt.
  • Activity grew fast in the first year: 177 transactions in January 1993 and 5,669 in December 1993, as more accounts opened.

Tools: SQL Server, Python, Power BI
